# GoFieldWise LeadLaunch Plumbing
## Dallas 4-Week Outbound + Nurture Calendar

This plan is designed for one sender mailbox and 100 new Dallas plumbing prospects per week.

## Weekly Throughput Targets
- New cold contacts: 100/week
- Follow-ups: 150-220/week
- Positive reply target: >= 2%
- Discovery call target: 2-4/week

## Daily Sending Windows (Dallas local time)
- Morning batch: 7:15 AM to 8:30 AM
- Afternoon batch: 4:30 PM to 6:00 PM

## Week 1
### Monday
- Import Segment A prospects (highest lead score)
- Send Email 1 to 20 contacts
- Verify bounce handling and auto-pauses

### Tuesday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Send Email 2 to Monday non-responders
- Reply to all inbound replies same day

### Wednesday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Send Email 2 to Tuesday non-responders
- Schedule discovery calls from interested replies

### Thursday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Send Email 2 to Wednesday non-responders
- Send Email 3 to Monday sequence cohort

### Friday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Send Email 2 to Thursday non-responders
- Weekly KPI review: opens, replies, positive replies, calls booked

## Week 2
### Monday
- Load next 100 prospects (mix Segment A/B)
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Send Email 3 to Tuesday week-1 cohort

### Tuesday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Send Email 2 to Monday non-responders
- Send Email 4 to Monday week-1 cohort

### Wednesday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Continue follow-up cadence (Email 2/3/4 by cohort date)
- Hold 2 outreach calls to warm replies

### Thursday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Continue follow-up cadence
- Send Email 5 to eligible week-1 cohort

### Friday
- Send Email 1 to 20 new contacts
- Continue follow-up cadence
- Pipeline cleanup and lost/nurture tagging

## Week 3
### Monday-Friday Rhythm
- 20 new contacts/day (Segment B/C mix)
- Trigger Email 6 for day-21 cohort exits
- Move interested-not-ready contacts to Nurture-Warm list
- Send weekly nurture newsletter to Nurture-Warm (value content only)

## Week 4
### Monday-Friday Rhythm
- Repeat Week 3 sending rhythm
- Run A/B test:
  - Subject A: quick idea for {{company_name}}
  - Subject B: missed calls from your site?
- Compare results and keep winner for next month

## Nurture-Warm Weekly Topics (8-week rotation)
1. Mobile conversion checklist for plumbing websites
2. Emergency service page blueprint
3. Before/after homepage copy examples
4. Missed-call text-back workflow
5. Local SEO basics for Dallas plumbers
6. Tracking booked jobs by source
7. FAQ: launch timelines and maintenance
8. Limited onboarding slot invitation

## CRM Update Rules
- Any reply -> remove from automated cold sequence immediately
- Positive reply -> stage "Replied - Interested"
- No response after Email 6 -> stage "Nurture-Warm" or "Closed - No Response"
- Bounced email -> stage "Data Fix Needed"

## Friday Review Checklist
- Reply rate by segment (A/B/C)
- Booked calls by segment
- Bounce rate and invalid contacts
- Top objections heard on calls
- Copy changes for next week
