# GoFieldWise.com Knowledge Base

## Pricing
- **GoFieldWise**: $200/month
- **GoFieldWise Pro**: $500/month

## Plan Names & Features

### GoFieldWise (Standard)
- AI Receptionist (voice and SMS)
- Call routing and number-based workflows
- Basic analytics dashboard
- Unlimited inbound calls
- Standard support (24hr SLA)

### GoFieldWise Pro
- All Standard features
- Advanced call analytics
- Custom call scripts and workflows
- CRM integration
- Priority support (24hr SLA)
- Dedicated onboarding

## Support
- **Support SLA**: 24 hours (responses within 1 business day)
- Support channels: Email, in-app chat, phone (Pro only)
- Knowledge base and troubleshooting guides available

## FAQ

**Q: What is GoFieldWise?**
A: GoFieldWise is an AI-powered receptionist platform for small businesses, handling inbound calls, routing, and customer support.

**Q: What numbers are supported?**
A: Both local and toll-free numbers. Your main numbers are 602 (front office) and 855 (sales/support).

**Q: How does call routing work?**
A: Calls are routed based on the number dialed, with dedicated flows for front office and sales/support.

**Q: What is the support response time?**
A: All plans include a 24-hour SLA for support requests.

**Q: Can I customize call scripts?**
A: Yes, Pro plan users can request custom call scripts and workflows.

**Q: How do I access analytics?**
A: Analytics are available in the dashboard for all plans; Pro users get advanced analytics.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Use the in-app chat or email for Standard; Pro users can also call the support line.
